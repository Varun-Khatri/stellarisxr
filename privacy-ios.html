Games privacy policy

iOS games

Last updated November 13, 2026. Also available: Android games policy.

Who we are

Stellaris XR Studios ("we", "us") is an independent game studio in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. This policy explains what information our mobile games distributed through the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad collect, why, who receives it and the choices you have. Questions or requests: varun@stellarisxr.com.

It covers these games:

GameStatusServices used
SporelightiOS version in developmentGoogle AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Apple In-App Purchase, Apple Game Center

The short version

  • Our games have no accounts and we run no servers. We do not see, store or sell your personal information.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device (and in your own device backup, if you have backup turned on).
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by the store. We never receive your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, unlocked items and purchase status are saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on, iOS may include this file in your backup so progress can be restored; that backup is stored by Apple under your Apple Account or on your computer. Deleting the game removes the file from the device.

Advertising: Google AdMob and App Tracking Transparency

Free versions of our games show ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect your IP address (used to estimate a rough location), device and operating system information, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data. iOS asks your permission before any app can use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not available and ads are not personalised using it.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent: Google User Messaging Platform

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a US state with a privacy law, the game shows Google's consent message before any ads load, followed where applicable by Apple's tracking permission prompt. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices in the game.

Purchases: Apple In-App Purchase

In-app purchases (for example, removing ads) are processed by Apple. Apple handles your payment details; the game only receives confirmation of what you own so it can unlock it, and checks that status again when the game starts so refunds are respected. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Leaderboards and achievements: Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple receives your Game Center profile, your leaderboard scores and achievements so they can be shown to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to friends or other players. Game Center is optional; the game is fully playable without it. Manage it in iOS Settings > Game Center.

Crash and performance data

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers on your device, Apple may provide us with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. We do not use any other analytics.

Your choices on iOS

  • Ad consent: Settings > Ad privacy choices in the game (shown where the law requires it).
  • Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, to allow or deny tracking for each app.
  • Personalised ads from Apple: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Fewer ads: the one-time PRO purchase removes banner and between-level ads.
  • Game Center: sign out in iOS Settings > Game Center.
  • Delete local data: delete the game from your device.

Legal bases (EU and UK players)

Personalised advertising and the related use of device identifiers rely on your consent, collected through Google's consent message. Showing non-personalised ads, preventing ad fraud, processing purchases and saving progress rely on our legitimate interests in running a free, ad-supported game and on performing our contract with you when you buy something.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, Canada, Quebec or a US state with a privacy law) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about players, most requests are best made directly to the provider that holds it (Google or Apple, linked above), and you can always change ad consent in the game. We are happy to help: email varun@stellarisxr.com and we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, or in Canada to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

We do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalised ads through AdMob may count as "sharing" or targeted advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out through the game's ad privacy choices or your device settings described above.

Children

Our games are intended for players aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through one of our games, contact varun@stellarisxr.com and we will help remove it.

Retention, transfers and security

We keep no player data on our own systems. Information collected by Google or Apple is kept for the periods described in their privacy policies and may be processed in the United States and other countries where they operate, under the safeguards described in those policies. All communication between the game and these services is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If our games start using a new service or collecting different information, we will update this page and the "last updated" date before that version is released.
